Checked 8 October 2026. This is a proposed website concept, not an approved official website.

## Identity and claims

- Lead sheet row 8: Smiles Boutique, Bangur / Lake Town, boutique / ethnic wear, phone +91 98741 43888. Existing Website field was blank.
- [Matching Google Maps listing](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Smiles+Boutique/@22.6055363,88.413067,17z/data=!4m15!1m8!3m7!1s0x3a0275f93fffffff:0x30044cccb901f7e9!2sSmiles+Boutique!8m2!3d22.6055117!4d88.4130762!10e5!16s%2Fg%2F1q62hcb1k!3m5!1s0x3a0275f93fffffff:0x30044cccb901f7e9!8m2!3d22.6055117!4d88.4130762!16s%2Fg%2F1q62hcb1k?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTAwNS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D): name, Bangur location, phone +91 98741 43888, 35-photo count, 52-review count and 4.9 rating as displayed when checked. Only ten photos and three reviews were accessible without Google sign-in.
- [Supplied Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/SmilesTheStore/): name Smiles Boutique; describes ethnic and casual wear and links smilesboutique.in. Its phone +91 99032 27778 and street number 58 conflict with the sheet/Google number and Google/Chotu street number 68/3; neither conflicting phone nor precise street number is published in this concept. The Facebook-linked website did not load through the research service when checked; this is not proof of a permanent outage.
- [Magicpin listing](https://magicpin.in/Kolkata/Bangur/Fashion/Smiles-Boutique/store/1cbb83): matches the lead phone and lists dress materials. Its public four-photo page was inspected, though those low-resolution images were not needed after the higher-quality verified Google photos were obtained. Its enquiry link uses a platform number and was not treated as the boutique’s WhatsApp.
- [Supplied Chotu listing](https://chotu.com/local/tailors-in-lake-town-kolkata-west-bengal-india/ct-18407745/) includes the business in Bangur. [Supplied IDBF link](https://kolkata.idbf.in/4191688/smiles-boutique) could not be opened in research.
- Google reviews visible without sign-in: Latika Gupta, Abhishek Poddar and Swagata Rudra, each shown as 5 stars and 5 months old on 8 Oct 2026. The site uses summaries rather than verbatim review text. Source: matching Google Maps listing above.
- WhatsApp was signed out during verification; the sheet and verified listing provide a telephone number but do not label it as WhatsApp. No unverified WhatsApp link was included.
